# Feather: Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Feather is a to-do app. This page says plainly what it does with your information. The short version: **it does not collect any.**

## What Feather collects
Nothing. Feather has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. The app does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

## Where your data is
Your tasks, tags, reminders and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

## What you can choose to do with your data
- **Export or back up.** Feather can write your tasks to a file (JSON or CSV) in a place you pick with the system file picker, and it can write a daily backup file into a folder you pick. These files are as private as the place you save them. If you pick a folder that a cloud service syncs (for example a Google Drive folder), that service handles the files under its own policy. Feather does not read or send them.
- **Share into Feather.** When you share text from another app, Feather receives only that text.
- **Voice input.** The microphone button uses your phone's own speech recognizer. Feather does not record audio and does not need the microphone permission. Depending on your phone, the recognizer may send what you say to its provider (often Google) to turn it into text; that is governed by the provider's policy and your phone's settings.
- **Android's own backup.** Android may back up Feather's task database and settings to your Google account, and copy them to a new phone, if you have those system features switched on. This is done by the operating system, not by Feather, and you control it in your phone's settings. Feather's backup rules limit it to the task database and the settings file.
- **Links.** The reminder helper can open a help page (dontkillmyapp.com) in your browser, only when you tap it.
- **Tips.** If you choose to leave a tip, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. Feather never sees your payment details, never stores that a tip was left and unlocks nothing because of it. Feather uses Google Play Billing only to show the amounts and to start the payment.

## Permissions Feather asks for
- **Notifications:** to show your reminders and the optional daily summary.
- **Alarms and reminders (exact alarms):** so a reminder arrives at the minute you set, not several minutes late. You can decline it; reminders then may be a little late.
- **Receive boot completed:** so reminders are set again after the phone restarts.

## Children
Feather does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published with the app and the date above will change.

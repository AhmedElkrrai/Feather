Feather is a to-do app that stays out of your way. It is completely free, has no ads, needs no account and keeps everything on your phone.

Type a task the way you would say it, "Call mom tomorrow 5pm !high #family", and Feather fills in the date, time, priority and tag for you. It understands English and Arabic, and you can speak it instead of typing.

What you get
• Tasks with notes, due dates and times, four priorities, checklists and tags, and tags can sit under other tags
• Today, Upcoming, Overdue, Goals and Completed views, tabs to filter by tag, and search
• Reminders that arrive on time: several per task, snooze, and Complete from the notification, plus a helper that shows what could stop reminders on your phone
• Repeating tasks: daily, weekly on chosen days, monthly (including "the 2nd Tuesday"), yearly, or counted from the day you finish
• Home-screen widgets: today's list, the next task, and a one-tap add button; a Quick Settings tile and a shortcut too
• Share text from any app straight into Feather
• Select several tasks at once, duplicate, and a trash that keeps deleted tasks for 30 days
• Light, dark and pure black themes, Material You colors, and full right-to-left support in English and Arabic

Your data is yours
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no internet permission
• Export everything to a file and import it again, or let Feather back up once a day into a folder you choose
• Free forever. Donations are welcome and never unlock anything: every feature is for everyone.

Reminders need a little care on some phones. Open Settings, then Reminder reliability, and Feather will show you what to allow.
